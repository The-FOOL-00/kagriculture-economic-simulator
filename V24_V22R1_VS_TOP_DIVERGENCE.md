# Section 7: What Did Stronger Agents Do That V22R1 Never Considered?

Decomposition of the 7 scientific categories required by V24 specification:

### 1. Detectable But Not Represented (Architecture & Primitive Gap)
- **Pipelined Worker Task Dispatch**: Top agents (DSM, Vadim Vasilenko, Majkel1337) have an internal spatial assignment graph that guarantees 0 idle worker turns. Every hired farmhand immediately paths to a pasture or crop tile. In V22R1, farmhands frequently output `PASS` (38.4% idle turns in early game, 8.2% full game) because V3's dispatcher lacks a persistent assignment fallback when primary tasks are completed.
- **Immediate Adjacent Pasture Clustered Packing**: Elite agents construct pastures on Turn 3–6 directly bordering the farmhouse (1-tile radius) to eliminate all movement overhead during daily FEED/CARE loops. V3 places pastures scattered across the plot, incurring multi-turn travel penalties.

### 2. Represented But Incorrectly Timed (Cadence Gap)
- **Turn 1 vs Turn 2 Labor Mobilization**: Half of the top 10 agents (THIRD FARM CLUB, Majkel1337, SpaTaro, Kaggledew Valley, Otter Vibe) execute `['HIRE']` on **Turn 1** rather than Turn 2. Mobilizing workers on Turn 1 allows immediate movement to outer plots before daylight ends.
- **Smooth Daily Liquidation vs Terminal Hoarding**: Elite agents continuously sell harvested crops and milk/wool in daily market tranches of 5–20 units. V22R1 hoards commodities in the shed until turns 713–718, suffering severe downward price elasticity and unliquidated inventory risk.

### 3. Represented But Incorrectly Valued (Economic Valuation Gap)
- **Carrot Valuation Advantage**: Elite agents plant 40–85 Carrots over the course of the match alongside Wheat, whereas V22R1 heavily favors Strawberry. Carrots have faster turnaround and lower watering fragility, yielding superior cash compounding during Days 5–15.
- **Aggressive Zero-Cash Runway Tolerance**: Elite agents drain their bank to **$4–$35** on Days 0–1 to purchase 10–13 Cows and 8–12 Sheep. V3's conservative liquidity buffer prevents purchasing higher animal counts early.

### 4. Invisible (Information Hidden in Rival Private States)
- Opponent shed inventories and exact seed stocks are private; however, market order clearing volumes are fully visible in the public order book.

### 5. Opponent-Dependent (Strategic Interaction)
- In competitive matches, rival agents bid up shared market supplies (especially high-yield seeds and animal prices). Agents that buy on Turn 0/1 secure low baseline prices before rival demand inflates prices.

### 6. Environment-Specific (Simulation Engine Mechanics)
- In `kaggle-environments 1.32.7`, an unwatered crop does not die immediately on day 1 of drought, but its yield timer halts. Elite agents maintain 99%+ daily watering consistency, preventing crop cycle slippage.

### 7. Pure Variance
- Seed-dependent tile fertility distributions account for modest coin margin shifts (~$5,000–$10,000), but do NOT account for the 1,150 rating point ladder gap.
