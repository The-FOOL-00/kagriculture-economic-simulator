## 1. Problem Definition & Architectural Purpose

In V3, worker movements and macro actions are generated via precomputed route tapes. When AEC modifies an individual worker action to exploit a tactical opportunity (such as digging an invasive weed, performing dynamic crop harvesting, or detouring around an obstacle), the remaining actions on the V3 route tape become spatially desynchronized. 

Previously, the counterfactual simulator correctly detected this discrepancy and failed closed:
$$\text{Spatial Divergence} \implies \text{Stale Continuation} \implies \text{Safety Rejection} \implies \text{Deterministic ABSTAIN}$$

While fail-closed behavior prevented corrupt execution, it imposed a severe structural bottleneck: **no worker route change could ever be committed**, blocking legitimate economic capabilities:
* `LOCAL_WEED_DIG`
* `DYNAMIC_SHADOW_HARVEST`
* `CARGO_FERRY_PRIORITY`
* `TERMINAL_HARVEST_SWEEP`
* Future Labor Shadow-Price Interventions

**AEC-RECONNECT** solves this problem by providing a deterministic route splicing compiler pass that computes the minimal valid bridge path to reconnect the diverged worker back to the unaffected baseline continuation, while propagating downstream temporal, inventory, and multi-worker dependencies.

## 2. Formal Trajectory Representations (§6)

The architecture establishes formal, typed dataclasses in `aec/replanning/reconnect.py`:

### 2.1 `WorkerTrajectoryState`
Represents the complete instantaneous physical and economic state of a worker:
* `worker_id: int` (0 for farmer, $1 \dots N$ for hired hands)
* `position: Tuple[int, int]` (exact board coordinate $[0, 9] \times [0, 9]$)
* `task: str` (current action / macro role)
* `target: Optional[Tuple[int, int]]` (intended waypoint)
* `route: List[str]` (planned directional move sequence)
* `eta: int` (steps remaining to target)
* `carried_inventory: Dict[str, int]` (units of each commodity held)
* `hand_usage: Dict[str, Any]` (co-worker allocation)
* `action_dependencies: List[str]` (precondition nodes)
* `downstream_waypoints: List[Tuple[int, int]]` (planned future coordinate sequence)
* `critical_obligations: List[Dict[str, Any]]` (delivery deadlines, feeding deadlines)
* `expected_resource_arrivals: Dict[str, Any]` (expected shed deliveries)

### 2.2 `TrajectorySegment`
Represents a temporal slice of a worker schedule:
* `start_state: WorkerTrajectoryState`
* `action_sequence: List[Dict[str, Any]]`
* `expected_end_state: WorkerTrajectoryState`
* `timing: int` (duration in steps)
* `spatial_occupancy: List[Tuple[int, Tuple[int, int]]]` (coordinate footprint over time)
* `resource_consequences: Dict[str, int]` (inventory delta)
* `market_consequences: Dict[str, Any]` (clearing impact)
* `obligation_consequences: Dict[str, Any]` (deadline shifts)

### 2.3 `ReconnectRequest`
Input contract submitted by the compiler or opportunity engine:
* `worker: int`
* `divergence_point: int` (game step where mutation occurs)
* `patched_action: List[str]` (new worker command)
* `intended_target_state: Optional[Tuple[int, int]]`
* `baseline_continuation: List[Dict[str, Any]]` (unmodified future backbone actions)
* `causal_closure: Set[str]` (transitive dependency set)
* `econ_state: EconomicState` (current game observation snapshot)
* `co_worker_trajectories: Dict[int, List[Tuple[int, int]]]` (other workers' spacetime paths)
* `scheduled_market_sales: List[Dict[str, Any]]` (locked downstream sale orders)
* `critical_obligations: List[Dict[str, Any]]` (hard deadlines)
* `remaining_steps: int`

### 2.4 `ReconnectResult`
Output certificate generated by `ReconnectionEngine`:
* `status: ReconnectionStatus`
* `path: List[str]` (bridge moves)
* `timing_shift: int` ($\Delta t \ge 0$)
* `affected_nodes: Set[str]`
* `affected_workers: Set[int]`
* `affected_resources: Dict[str, int]`
* `fidelity: str` (`"VERIFIED"`, `"PARTIAL"`, `"FAILED"`)
* `safety: bool` (`True` if valid, `False` if fail-closed)
* `reconnect_waypoint_index: int` (continuation offset where suffix resumes)
* `reconnect_target_pos: Tuple[int, int]` (grid coordinate where bridge meets suffix)
* `spliced_trajectory: List[Dict[str, Any]]` (executable composite continuation)
* `escalation_scope: EscalationScope`
* `rejection_reason: Optional[str]`

## 3. Movement Semantics & Constrained Pathfinding (§7)

Movement follows the exact discrete grid physics of the competition engine:
* **Grid Topology:** $10 \times 10$ board ($0 \le x < 10$, $0 \le y < 10$).
* **Directional Vectors:**
  - `NORTH`: $(0, -1)$
  - `SOUTH`: $(0, 1)$
  - `EAST`: $(1, 0)$
  - `WEST`: $(-1, 0)$
  - `PASS`: $(0, 0)$
* **Deterministic Tie-Breaking:** Canonical search order `["NORTH", "SOUTH", "EAST", "WEST"]`. Zero stochasticity, zero random jitter.
* **Spacetime Reservation Table (`SpacetimeReservationTable`):**
  - **Vertex Occupancy:** $P_i(t) \ne P_j(t)$ for distinct workers $i \ne j$ at any step $t$.
  - **Edge Swap Protection:** Prevents head-on swap collisions where worker $i$ moves $u \to v$ while worker $j$ moves $v \to u$ at step $t$:
    $$P_i(t) = P_j(t-1) \land P_j(t) = P_i(t-1) \implies \text{REJECTED}$$

## 4. Reconnection Target Selection (§9)

Rather than naively forcing reconnection to the immediate next waypoint, `ReconnectionEngine` scans the future continuation tape and identifies candidate targets:
1. **Immediate Next Waypoint:** First scheduled continuation step.
2. **Task Boundaries:** Grid coordinates where the worker is scheduled to perform a tile operation (`HARVEST`, `PLANT`, `WATER`, `DIG`, `FEED`, `CARE`).
3. **Resource Dependency Boundaries:** Shed delivery tiles (`DROP`, `STORE`).
4. **Macro Checkpoints:** Day transition boundaries (step multiples of 24) or idle rest waypoints.

### Ranking & Selection Metric
Viable paths are evaluated and ranked via:
1. **Exact Timing Parity ($\Delta t = 0$):** Targets reachable on or before the scheduled time receive highest priority (`SAFE_RECONNECT`).
2. **Task & Resource Boundaries:** Targets corresponding to macro tasks are preferred over arbitrary intermediate corridor moves.
3. **Minimal Timing Shift:** If delayed, the target with minimal $\Delta t$ is selected.
4. **Earliest Continuation Offset:** Minimizes replanning scope.

## 5. Route Splicing Mechanics (§8, §13)

The spliced trajectory is assembled as:
$$\text{Spliced Trajectory} = \text{PREFIX} + \text{PATCHED ACTION} + \text{BRIDGE PATH} + \text{BASELINE SUFFIX}$$

1. **At Step 0 (Current Step):** Patched action is executed by the mutated worker; co-workers execute their baseline actions.
2. **At Steps $1 \dots L_{\text{bridge}}$:** Mutated worker executes bridge moves generated by the constrained pathfinder; co-workers continue their baseline continuation actions.
3. **From Step $L_{\text{bridge}} + 1$ Onward:** Baseline suffix actions resume from the reconnection target. If the target was a tile operation (e.g. `HARVEST` or `DROP`), the operation is included in the suffix so the worker executes the planned task upon arrival.

## 6. Multi-Tier Adaptive Escalation (§5, §10)

The fixed 4-step cutoff from Checkpoint 001 fixtures is replaced with dynamic causal escalation:

| Level | Name | Trigger Conditions | Max Horizon | Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | `LOCAL` | Bridge length $\le 1$, $\Delta t = 0$, closure size $\le 1$ | 4 steps | Tactical single-worker detour |
| **Level 2** | `DEPENDENCY_CLOSURE` | Bridge length $\le 3$, $\Delta t \le 2$, single worker closure (size $2 \dots 4$) | 8 steps | Reconnect with slight schedule adjustment |
| **Level 3** | `MEDIUM_REPLAN` | Timing shift impacts shed delivery or market order timing, closure $3 \dots 5$ | 18 steps | Cross-subsystem worker-market coordination |
| **Level 4** | `EXTENDED_REPLAN` | Multi-worker coordination needed, livestock lifecycle impact, closure $\ge 5$ | 36 steps | Multi-worker schedule deconfliction |
| **Level 5** | `FULL_REPLAN` | Terminal endgame proximity (remaining steps $\le 24$) or unresolvable conflict | End of Game | Full tactical/macro re-evaluation |

## 7. Safety Certificate Extension (§15)

`SafetyCertificate` and `SafetyChecker` are extended with 9 explicit invariants:
1. `spatial_validity_check`: Zero out-of-bounds moves; valid grid navigation.
2. `timing_validity_check`: Downstream deadlines (market sales, feed obligations) preserved.
3. `route_continuity_check`: Bridge path connects diverged point to target waypoint continuously.
4. `worker_consistency_check`: All active worker IDs exist and have valid topology.
5. `resource_consistency_check`: Zero shed overflow discards.
6. `obligation_consistency_check`: Intermediate market solvency and animal care preserved.
7. `market_consistency_check`: Zero order queue overflow; prices non-negative.
8. `determinism_check`: Exact reproducible rollout.
9. `counterfactual_fidelity`: Marked `VERIFIED`. Any `FAILED` or `UNKNOWN` fails closed to `ABSTAIN`.
