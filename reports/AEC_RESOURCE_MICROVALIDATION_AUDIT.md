## 3. Scientific Defect Audit: Step 697 Terminal Blindness

### Incident Description:
On Seeds 50004, 50013, and 50018, the compiler committed an `EMERGENCY_FEED_PURCHASE` at turn 697.
- **Active Trigger Flags:** `['E4_MARKET_REGIME_SHIFT', 'E5_OPPONENT_SUPPLY_SHOCK', 'E8_TERMINAL_TRANSITION']`.
- **Game State Context:** Total match length is 720 turns. At turn 697, exactly 23 turns remained.
- **Defect Mechanism:**
  - The `ResourceOpportunityAnalyzer` detected a projected feed deficit based on living animal count.
  - Unlike `ConversionDiscoveryEngine`, which evaluates terminal liquidation horizons, `ResourceOpportunityAnalyzer` lacked an early-abort check for `turn >= 720 - terminal_horizon`.
  - It emitted a buy order for wheat at market prices.
  - The wheat arrived too late to produce economic return, drained liquid cash prior to game conclusion, and occupied market transaction bandwidth.
  - Realized outcome: Seed 50004 suffered a net swing of `-$3,388.00` in terminal margin.

### Invariant Conformance Assessment:
- **Case EF-9 Failure:** The preregistration explicit case EF-9 states: *"Feed purchase near terminal: Terminal-aware logic prevents pointless/unexecutable action. Expected: ABSTAIN."*
- **Empirical Verdict:** **FAILED.** The mechanism committed rather than abstaining near terminal.
