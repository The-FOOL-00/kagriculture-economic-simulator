## 1. Epistemic Classification Rules

To prevent unfounded claims, speculation, or retracted theories from contaminating development, every finding in this project is classified into exactly one of five epistemic tiers:

1. **`ESTABLISHED FACT` (Tier A & B)**: Proven directly from primary source code (`sim.hpp`, `kaggriculture.py`) or verified bit-for-bit through deterministic simulation reproduction across full datasets. Can be treated as ground truth.
2. **`BOUNDED EMPIRICAL FINDING` (Tier C)**: Observed under explicitly evaluated parameter grids, seed cohorts, or partition boundaries. Must NOT be extrapolated beyond stated conditions.
3. **`INFERENCE` (Tier D)**: Mechanistic explanation consistent with observed evidence, but not yet experimentally confirmed through controlled counterfactual isolation.
4. **`HYPOTHESIS` (Tier E)**: Plausible theoretical mechanism or proposed strategy that has NOT yet been validated. Must be tested before inclusion in agent logic.
5. **`RETRACTED` (Tier F)**: Disproven, cherry-picked, or flawed claim. Permanently banned from future development logic.

## 3. Evidence Governance Rules

1. Any claim not cataloged in this ledger is classified as an **Unverified Hypothesis** and cannot be used to justify architectural or algorithmic decisions.
2. A claim may only be promoted from `HYPOTHESIS` to `BOUNDED EMPIRICAL FINDING` upon completing pre-registered evaluation on Discovery and Qualification partitions.
3. Promotion to `ESTABLISHED FACT` requires primary source code verification or bit-exact multi-partition replication with $p < 0.01$ and zero confidence interval overlap with zero.
