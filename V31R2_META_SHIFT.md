## What changed since early September

1. V3-lineage CONVERGENCE: early-September tapes (fixed routes, wheat
   cycling, closed-loop rules) are gone from the matched pool; the
   1800+ band is uniformly V3-reactive now. V30's gauntlet opponents
   (P3090/P3000/NIGHT/OKAMI/V66/L2530/ANDREY/MELON/SOIL) do not appear
   in 321 live games — that meta is extinct.
2. Competition moved INSIDE the V3 regime: edges are now wash-ledgers
   (±$600), livestock-mix (±$9k), fert/feed micro (±$10k),
   sell-execution micro (±$4k) — all invisible to V30-style
   macro-gauntlets (which V3 sweeps 176-0 precisely BECAUSE the
   proxies lack these adaptations).
3. No engine change (1.32.7 throughout). No new crops/shop mechanics
   observed. The shift is OPPONENT COMPOSITION, not environment.
