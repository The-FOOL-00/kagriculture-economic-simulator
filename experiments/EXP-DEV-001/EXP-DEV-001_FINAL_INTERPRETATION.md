## 2. What EXP-DEV-001 Proved

1. **Unhedged Early Crop Cannibalization Creates Unacceptable Tail Risk**:
   Replacing 13 primary strawberry cash crops with specialty crops on Day 11 based on only two shop reveals commits 40% of the farm's secondary crop capacity to a directional bet before the majority of town demand (5 of 8 shops) is known.
2. **The Treatment Was Highly Inert Across the General Distribution**:
   Because Priority 1 (`YARN`) and Priority 2 (`STRAWBERRY`) mapped back to `ClosedLoopAgentV2`, and because the joint probability of drawing at least one Yarn or Strawberry shop across Days 3 and 6 is $55/64 = 85.94\%$, the candidate executed actions 100% bit-exact identical to V2 on 42 of 50 seeds (84.0%).
3. **The Reported Positive Mean (+265.18) Is Statistically Fragile**:
   The arithmetic mean is not statistically significant ($p = 0.3963$, 95% CI `[-$357.51, +$887.87]`) and is 100% dependent on a single positive outlier (Seed 50004). Excluding that single seed turns the mean negative (-$40.92); the 10% trimmed mean is +$0.40.

## 3. What EXP-DEV-001 Did NOT Prove

1. **It Did NOT Prove That "Dynamic Adaptation Is Fundamentally Inferior"**:
   The failure disconfirms early, unhedged replacement of core crops based on premature shop signals. It does not test:
   - Additive secondary production utilizing physical spatial slack (vacant tiles) without cannibalizing core strawberries (the subject of `EXP-DEV-003`).
   - Hedged adaptation conditioning on late-game shop reveals (Days 12–15) when shop uncertainty is reduced.
   - Dynamic liquidity hedging (the subject of `EXP-DEV-004`).
2. **It Did NOT Prove That "Tomatoes Are Intrinsically Inferior"**:
   On Seed 50004, the exact same 13 Tomato plants generated +$15,264.00 in margin when town demand matched the crop. Tomatoes are an effective cash crop when town demand exists, but substituting them unconditionally creates severe downside volatility when town demand pivots to strawberries.
3. **It Did NOT Involve Animal Starvation or Feed Buffer Failure**:
   All initial narrative claims that Seed 50018 failed due to Pet Cafe, Yarn Store, carrot diversion, or animal starvation are formally retracted as factually disproven by the simulation traces.
