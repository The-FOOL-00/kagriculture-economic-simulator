## The V49 Position Problem

V49 is confirmed to be a **post-carrot-revolution September-route agent**.  
V58 established: V49 beats ALL August 3100+ routes (0/400 tournament legs lost).

**But this does NOT establish:** V49 ≥ Sep 22 3000+ agents.

The Sep 22 3000+ teams submitted their agents on **Sep 21–22** — i.e., they are  
ACTIVE current-meta submissions, not fossils. These agents have NEVER been tested  
against V49 from local replay data.

**The gap question:**
- V49 controlled benchmark: 99.31% win rate vs local test suite
- DSM (Sep 22): 3109.5 leaderboard rating  
- If V49 maps to approximately ~2800 live rating → gap ≈ **300 rating points**
- A 300-point gap at the top of a Kaggle competition is **material** and strategic

## Strategy Evolution Hypothesis

Given that massive rating gains occurred Sep 1–22 for multiple teams  
WITHOUT access to their replay data, two mechanisms are possible:

**Hypothesis A:** Same V49-style macro route, better execution/parameters.  
→ Gap is micro, not strategic. Counter-strategy approach would find nothing.

**Hypothesis B:** New macro strategy emerged in September that V49 has not yet played.  
→ Early branching could be valuable IF the strategy can be identified early.

**V59 cannot distinguish A from B** without September replay data.  
The correct scientific response is to attempt what IS possible:  
1. Test if V49 has a known weakness in its current micro execution  
2. Test what the destbreso Aug benchmark reveals about our performance ceiling  
3. Document the gap analysis for future data acquisition
