# V53 COWSWAP Regime Report: The Adversarial Duopoly Discovery (Section 5)

## 1. Executive Summary

Our turn-by-turn counterfactual analysis across discovery seeds and historical loss matches has uncovered the exact, definitive causal mechanism behind the ~$1,400 loss cluster against V44 (L07..L10 on seeds 250436 and 250439):

**The "Adversarial Market Duopoly" Effect**:
- When COWSWAP activates, V49 switches from Cows to Geese because egg production yields higher *isolated absolute revenue* (+496 on 250436, +$1,316 on 250439).
- However, by vacating the milk market, V49 grants `V44` an **uncontested monopoly** on high-margin Milk ($160/unit). Under this monopoly, V44 earns over **+$4,100 additional revenue**, allowing V44 to surpass V49 by ~$1,400.
- When COWSWAP is suppressed, V49 stays in Cows. Even though V49 earns slightly less absolute money (-$496 on 250436, -$1,316 on 250439), V49's milk supply **depresses the shared town market price of Milk from $160 down to $110**.
- This market price collapse depresses V44's revenue by **-$4,350.00** on Seed 250436 and **-$4,141.00** on Seed 250439, flipping both matches from decisive losses into decisive wins (+2,449 on 250436, +$1,418 on 250439).

## 3. Why Global Suppression is Fatal

Across the general meta:
1. When opponents are not running heavy dairy (or against adversaries and lower-tier bots), COWSWAP provides an average of **+$1,200 to +$3,400 pure positive alpha** (as seen on seeds 250413, 250414, 250415, 250419, 250425).
2. Globally suppressing COWSWAP destroys this alpha, incurring **30 casualties** on discovery seeds and **4 casualties** out of sample (-$158.02 mean delta, -$448.76 elite delta).
3. Therefore, COWSWAP must only be suppressed if we can detect that the rival is actively scaling dairy production or if market saturation dynamics dictate adversarial price depression.
