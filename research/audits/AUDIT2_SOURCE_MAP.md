## 2. Critical Methodological Findings on Audit #1's Evidence Selection

### Finding 1: Audit #1 Inherited a Broken Replay Parser for S2
In claiming that `episode_107543042.json` proved a "Day-12 hiring collapse where hands dropped from 7 to 4 because cash < $800":
- Audit #1 cited `scratch/reconstructed_live_losses_forensics.json`.
- That JSON was produced by `scratch/reconstruct_live_losses.py`, which attempted to parse worker hands via `f.get("units", [])`.
- In the official Kaggle environment, hands are stored under the key `f["hands"]`, NOT `f["units"]`.
- Consequently, `reconstruct_live_losses.py` reported `hands: 0` for all checkpoints across all replays.
- Audit #1 never independently checked the true replay `f["hands"]` array or the step-by-step cash balance on Day 12 ($9,847), nor did it check the engine hiring cost formula (`cfg.hire_mult * fib(hires_today)`).
- Audit #1 repeated an author's narrative that was completely contradicted by the raw replay.

### Finding 2: Audit #1 Confused "Same Base Tape" with "Bit-for-Bit Identical Physical Agronomy" for S4
Audit #1 made the absolute claim that V2 and Dmitrii execute "100.0% bit-for-bit identical physical agronomy across all 30 days":
- While V2 and Dmitrii instantiate the same 13 base agronomy tapes, physical actions depend on resource affordability at runtime.
- On Seed 50081 (the worst loss seed of -$730), V2 entered Step 217 with $950.0 cash versus Dmitrii's $954.0 (a tiny $4 deficit caused by early wool sales).
- At Step 217, both agents attempted to buy 2 Sheep (`['BUY_ANIMAL', 'SHEEP', 2]`, cost $1,000) and hire a worker. Dmitrii could afford both; V2 fell $3 short of the second sheep.
- At Step 259, Hand 6 had no sheep to place on Tile (1, 4).
- For the remainder of the game (460 turns), Tile (1, 4) was an empty pasture on V2's farm, while Dmitrii had a producing sheep on Tile (1, 4).
- Furthermore, at steps 712–717, Dmitrii executes a dynamic 64-sim Monte Carlo terminal planner (`ShopClosure`) that moves hands and collects fertilizer, whereas V2 executes static tape actions (`PASS`).
- Audit #1's assertion of 100.0% physical identity was an unsubstantiated overclaim.
