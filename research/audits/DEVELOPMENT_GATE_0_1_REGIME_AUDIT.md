## 1. Primary Audit Verdict on World Regimes

### **VERDICT: READY WITH CORRECTIONS**

### **Core Finding**:
`DEVELOPMENT_WORLD_REGIMES.md` correctly quotes the underlying simulator shop table and draw mechanics. However, an adversarial audit reveals two material epistemic issues:

## 2. Mathematical Audit of Town Shop Demand Probabilities

### 2.1 Simulator Draw Mechanics Verification
- In `sim.hpp:135`, town shops are instantiated via:
  ```cpp
  rng.choice_index(N_SHOPS); // where N_SHOPS = 8
  ```
- **Sampling Mechanism**: Exactly 8 shop instances are sampled **uniform at random with replacement** across 8 unlock events (Days 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24).
- Total possible shop sequences: $8^8 = 16,777,216$ equally likely permutations.

### 2.2 Marginal Draw Probability vs. 8-Draw Episode Occurrence

Let $p_c$ denote the probability that a single drawn shop demands commodity $c$.  
Because draws are independent and identically distributed with replacement, the probability that commodity $c$ appears **at least once** in an 8-draw episode is:
$$\mathbb{P}(\text{At Least 1 Draw in 8}) = 1 - (1 - p_c)^8$$
And the probability of appearing in the first 3 reveals (by Day 9) is:
$$\mathbb{P}(\text{At Least 1 Draw in First 3}) = 1 - (1 - p_c)^3$$

| Commodity | Demanding Shops (Count / 8) | Single Slot Marginal Draw Probability ($p_c$) | Probability in First 3 Reveals (by Day 9) | Probability Across Full 8-Shop Episode | Expected Shop Count in 8 Draws ($\mu = 8 p_c$) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Wheat** | Bakery, Brunch, Farmers Market, Ice Cream, Pizza (5) | **62.50% (5/8)** | **94.73%** | **99.96%** | 5.00 shops |
| **Strawberry** | Brunch, Farmers Market, Ice Cream, Smoothie (4) | **50.00% (4/8)** | **87.50%** | **99.61%** | 4.00 shops |
| **Milk** | Ice Cream, Pizza, Smoothie (3) | **37.50% (3/8)** | **75.59%** | **97.67%** | 3.00 shops |
| **Tomato** | Farmers Market, Pizza (2) | **25.00% (2/8)** | **57.81%** | **89.99%** | 2.00 shops |
| **Carrot** | Farmers Market, Pet Cafe (2) | **25.00% (2/8)** | **57.81%** | **89.99%** | 2.00 shops |

| **Wool** | Yarn Store (1) | **12.50% (1/8)** | **33.01%** | **65.64%** | 1.00 shops |

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Audit Takeaway**: 
> - A marginal draw probability of $25.0\%$ for Tomato does **NOT** mean Tomato worlds are rare. Tomato appears in **9 out of every 10 full matches (89.99%)**!
> - Strawberry appears in **255 out of every 256 full matches (99.61%)**!
> - The true economic differentiator between matches is not *whether* a commodity appears, but **when** it appears (Day 3 vs Day 24) and **how many instances** appear.

## 3. Audit of the Four Proposed "World Regimes"

In `DEVELOPMENT_WORLD_REGIMES.md:48-128`, four regimes were proposed:
- **Regime A**: Straw-Rich ($\ge 2$ Straw in first 3 reveals)
- **Regime B**: Tomato / Pizza Synergy (Pizza in first 2 reveals + FM or Milk)
- **Regime C**: High-Multiplier Niche (Pet Cafe or Yarn Store in first 2 reveals)
- **Regime D**: Low-Strawberry Adverse (0 Straw in first 3 reveals)

### 3.1 Are These Simulator Game States or Analyst Abstractions?
- **Finding**: They are **100% analyst-created heuristic abstractions**.
- The simulator (`sim.hpp`) contains no state variable, enum, or transition logic corresponding to these regimes.
- The simulator only tracks an array of unlocked shop enums: `unlocked_shops`.

### 3.2 Regime Overlap & Non-Exclusivity
The four proposed regimes are **heavily overlapping**:
- **Example Seed**:
  - Step 72 (Day 3): `PET_CAFE` (Matches Regime C)
  - Step 144 (Day 6): `PIZZA_SHOP` (Matches Regime B)
  - Step 216 (Day 9): `BRUNCH_SPOT` (Demands Straw, Wheat, Egg)
  - Step 288 (Day 12): `SMOOTHIE_SHOP` (Demands Straw, Milk)
- Under the proposed rules, this seed simultaneously belongs to **Regime B (Pizza by Day 6)**, **Regime C (Pet Cafe by Day 6)**, and **Regime A ($\ge 2$ Straw by Day 12)**!
- Because they are non-exclusive, they cannot be used as an orthogonal experimental partition.

### 3.3 Observability and Temporal Horizons
- At **Day 0 (Step 0)**, exactly **0 shops are revealed**. All four regimes have zero observable support.
- At **Day 3 (Step 72)**, exactly **1 shop is revealed**. An agent cannot distinguish Regime A from Regime B or D with certainty.
- At **Day 6 (Step 144)**, exactly **2 shops are revealed**. An agent still cannot confirm Regime A ($\ge 2$ in 3 reveals) if only 1 Straw shop has appeared.
- At **Day 9 (Step 216)**, exactly **3 shops are revealed**. 5 of the 8 shops (62.5%) remain completely unknown.
- **Rule**: Any strategic decision conditioned on these regimes must be explicitly labeled as **probabilistic heuristic conditioning under partial observation**, never as a fixed "world type".

### Audit Discoveries:
1. **Post-Game Hindsight**: The partition was constructed by inspecting `obs_final` after Step 719, when all 8 shops had already been revealed.
2. **String Mismatch Bug**: The script looked for `"YARN_SHOP"`. In `sim.hpp:134`, the shop enum name is `YARN_STORE`. Consequently, `has_yarn` was **always False**, causing Yarn-demanding worlds to be miscategorized into `Straw` or `Pizza+Straw`.
3. **Straw Omnipresence**: Because Strawberry shops appear in $99.61\%$ of all 8-shop games, almost every seed contains at least one Strawberry shop by Step 719. Thus, seeds without Pizza were labeled `Straw` (50 seeds), and seeds with Pizza were labeled `Pizza+Straw` (99 seeds). Only 1 seed in 150 had Pizza without any Strawberry shop (`Pizza`, Seed 50042).

In `DEVELOPMENT_BASELINE_MAP.md` and `DEVELOPMENT_WORLD_REGIMES.md`, this breakdown must be documented as an **empirical post-hoc classification from the historical margin scan**, with explicit note of the `"YARN_SHOP"` string discrepancy, rather than an authoritative ontology of the game.
